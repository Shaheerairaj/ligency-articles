## Core thesis (client's framing)
- Not simply "China has caught up with the U.S."
- Fairer framing: Chinese labs turned open-weight models into a serious alternative to closed frontier AI by moving fast, releasing often, competing on benchmarks, and making models cheap and easy to adopt.
- The frontier gap narrowed on visible benchmarks but hasn't disappeared in real-world reliability, product polish, safety, tool use, assistant robustness.
- Open models = default for cost-sensitive automation, on-prem, sovereign AI, research, fine-tuning, niche enterprise.
- Closed models still dominate for the strongest general assistant, best agent loops, most reliable performance on messy tasks.
