# The Karpathy Loop: AI's Autonomous Research Revolution

**Source:** https://fortune.com/2026/03/17/andrej-karpathy-loop-autonomous-ai-agents-future/  
**Publication:** Fortune | **Date:** March 17, 2026

---

## The Experiment

Karpathy's AI coding agent ran continuously for **two days**, conducting **700 distinct experiments** to enhance small language model training. Results:
- Discovered **20 optimizations** that accelerated training time
- Applying tweaks to a larger model yielded an **11% speedup**
- Shopify CEO Tobias Lütke ran **37 experiments overnight** → **19% performance improvement** on internal company data

---

## Key Innovation: The Three-Part Formula

The system operates on three core components:
1. An agent accessing a **modifiable file**
2. A **measurable optimization metric**
3. **Fixed experiment time limits**

Industry analyst Janakiram MSV coined the term **"the Karpathy Loop"** — emphasizing its applicability far beyond AI research to any domain with a scoreable objective.

---

## Future Vision: From One Agent to a Research Community

Karpathy envisions scaling autoresearch through **collaborative agent swarms** exploring parallel optimizations simultaneously:

> "The goal is not to emulate a single PhD student, it's to emulate an entire research community working asynchronously."

Any efficiently-evaluable metric becomes viable for agent optimization — potentially transforming problem-solving across industries.

---

## Distinction from AutoML

Critics noted similarities to AutoML. Karpathy's counter:

| AutoML / NAS | AutoResearch |
|---|---|
| Random variations or evolutionary algorithms | LLM-based agent learns from previous experiments |
| No internet access or reasoning | Access to internet, prior results, and code context |
| Blind search | Structured hypothesis → implement → evaluate cycle |

The LLM-based approach "learns from previous experiments and internet access, representing substantially more sophisticated autonomous research capability."
